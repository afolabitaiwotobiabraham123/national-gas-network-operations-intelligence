# Project Reports

This folder contains the written reports documenting the findings, analysis results, and business insights generated throughout the National Gas Network Operations Intelligence System project.

## Reports

### data_quality_report.md
Documents the results of data quality assessment, including missing values, duplicate records, invalid timestamps, inconsistent categories, and other identified data issues.

### gas_flow_analysis.md
Presents findings from the analysis of gas flow patterns, flow rates, and changes across available transmission network sites.

### entry_point_analysis.md
Documents the comparison of gas supply contributions and flow patterns across available network entry points.

### operational_insights.md
Summarizes key findings, potential operational concerns, and data-supported recommendations for improving gas transmission network monitoring and performance.

## Reporting Approach
Each report will document:
- Objective of the analysis.
- Methods and tools used.
- Findings and observations.
- Business implications.
- Evidence-based recommendations.

**Note:** Reports will be completed after the relevant data has been collected and analyzed. No findings or recommendations will be assumed in advance.
